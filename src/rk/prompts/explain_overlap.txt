You are helping a researcher find out whether the idea in their research proposal has already been published. A literature search found the $n papers below, each with a number, its title and its abstract. Compare the proposal with each paper, facet by facet.

Judge meaning, not wording: published work often uses different terms from the proposal, and a method described without its name is the same method. The proposal is a plan written before the work, so its claim is an aim or expectation.

The four facets:
- problem: the research problem or question
- method: the method or approach
- domain: the domain or population studied, including any language, region, data source or user group
- claim: the main claimed contribution or finding

For each paper:
1. facets: for each facet, "same" if the paper's facet is the proposal's, "different" if the paper changes it, leaves it out, or is much broader or narrower. Give the reason in at most 15 words.
2. overlap: "high" if the paper already does what the proposal plans: the same problem, method and claim, in the same or a very close domain. "partial" if it shares the problem or the method but differs in another facet that matters. "low" if it shares only the broad topic.
3. evidence: one sentence copied exactly, character for character, from that paper's abstract: the sentence that best shows how the paper relates to the proposal. Do not add quotation marks, and do not shorten, join or change sentences.
4. differs: one line, at most 25 words, on what the paper does differently from the proposal. If nothing important differs, say so.

Proposal: $proposal

Papers:
$papers

Answer with JSON only, one entry per paper in the order of their numbers, in exactly this form:
{"papers": [{"paper": 1, "facets": {"problem": {"verdict": "same", "reason": "..."}, "method": {"verdict": "different", "reason": "..."}, "domain": {"verdict": "same", "reason": "..."}, "claim": {"verdict": "same", "reason": "..."}}, "overlap": "partial", "evidence": "...", "differs": "..."}]}
